    Other makes

    A car or bike of another make is private: only its owner sees it, in their garage, where they can still build its mods list and use it in Track sessions. It is not shown in the Gallery, the Reel or Build of the Week. When an owner asks for one to be shown, it waits here. Make public puts its photos in the Gallery and the Reel (Voting stays off until the owner enters a photo); Keep private leaves it as it is. Either way the owner is emailed.

    Other makes still on public view

    Cars of another make that anyone can still see: ones added before only Teslas went in, such as an Ioniq or a Taycan, and ones you made public (marked with the date). Remove from public view takes the car's photos out of the Gallery, the Reel and this week's vote (clearing its votes this week). Nothing is deleted: the car stays in its owner's garage with its photos, mods list and Track sessions, and they can ask for it to be shown again. With Email the owner on, its owner is told why.

    Members

    Subscribers, and messages to them.

    Subscribers

    Emails the list of members to modtesla3uk@gmail.com. It also goes out every day at 8pm.

    Messages to subscribers

    Members and subscribers are the same people: everyone with an MT3UK account. A message always goes to their Profile inbox, and the bell shows it as unread. Tick Also send it by email to email it too (members who have turned emails off are skipped).

    Emails this title and message to one address, with [Test] in the subject. Nothing is saved or sent to anyone else. To find what sends it to Junk, send it each way and see which arrive in the inbox.

    Reported friend messages

    Members can report a message a friend sent them. Remove deletes that message; Block also stops the sender messaging anyone until you unblock them.

    Interview previews

    Until an interview is published, anyone can ask for a one-time code on its page. A code opens that interview for 4 hours, signs them in, and makes them a member if they weren’t already. Below is everyone who has opened one. Revoke ends their preview straight away and stops new codes; Restore undoes it.

    Sharing links

    The picture a shared link shows in WhatsApp, Facebook and the rest, for the homepage. The Track sessions link picture is on the Track admin page.

    Homepage sharing

    The picture a shared homepage link shows in WhatsApp, Facebook and the rest. Pick a photo from the build gallery, upload one, or draw a card from a saved track session, give it a caption, and either rotate through them a week at a time or pick one. With none saved the link keeps its usual picture. Previews update within a few minutes of a change; a link already sent in a chat keeps its old picture.
