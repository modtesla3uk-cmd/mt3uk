    Members

    Subscribers, and messages to them.

    Subscribers

    Emails the list of members to modtesla3uk@gmail.com. It also goes out every day at 8pm.

    Messages to subscribers

    Members and subscribers are the same people: everyone with an MT3UK account. A message always goes to their Profile inbox, and the bell shows it as unread. Tick Also send it by email to email it too (members who have turned emails off are skipped).

    Emails this title and message to one address, with [Test] in the subject. Nothing is saved or sent to anyone else. To find what sends it to Junk, send it each way and see which arrive in the inbox.

    Reported friend messages

    Members can report a message a friend sent them. Remove deletes that message; Block also stops the sender messaging anyone until you unblock them.
