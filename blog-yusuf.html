Flik: Yusuf’s Blue Model 3 Long Range

Yusuf swapped a manual Seat Leon for a rare-spec blue Model 3 Long Range without a test drive. One MT3UK meet later, the modding had begun.

Who are you?

My name is Yusuf. I’m based in the West Midlands, and I’ve been in the group for just under 12 months.

What do you drive?

A 2021 Tesla Model 3 Long Range in blue, with a white interior and the upgraded Sport wheels.

Was this your first EV? What made you choose a Tesla?

Yes, it was my first EV. I came from a manual petrol Seat Leon. I looked at loads of other cars, from Audi, BMW, Mercedes and VW, but realised that for the same money I could get a Tesla.

I have a keen interest in technology, so I did loads of research. Of the BMW i4, the Polestar 2 and the Tesla Model 3, the Model 3 offered the best experience as a whole, including its fantastic Supercharger network. I also had to wait for this particular spec, which is quite rare.

What was buying it like?

Very nerve-racking, as there was no option to test drive the car. You just picked it up on collection day and drove it home if you were happy with its condition.

I had to wait two weeks for the car to be ready, and I was so nervous. I’m glad to say the inspection was perfect and the spec was fantastic. The first time I saw it in the flesh, the unique blue was striking, in a good way.

Pulling up at the house in this beast of a car without having told anyone about it was definitely memorable!

Why did you start modifying?

It was down to my first MT3UK car meet and meeting all the great people there. It gave me ideas for the future. I hadn’t been aware of the Tesla modding scene before that.

What was your first mod?

Tinting the rear windows.

Talk us through your mods so far.

  • Window tints: fitted by my local garage
  • Enhance Auto S3XY Strip: which I fitted myself

What’s your favourite mod, and why?

The S3XY Strip, because I installed it myself and it was straightforward and easy to fit.

Which mod gave you the best value for money?

The window tinting.

Is there anything you’d do differently?

Nothing for now.

How do you use the car?

At weekends and for family time, and for car shows with MT3UK when they’re local.

Has the car taken you anywhere memorable?

It’s my first automatic, and I’ve taken it down to London and to Wales.

What reactions do you get from other people?

People like the car, and the rare spec.

How did you find MT3UK?

Through a random Google search. It’s a fantastic group, with people always looking to offer help and guidance.

What’s your favourite MT3UK moment so far?

My first meet. I was made to feel very welcome, and the group is very friendly, online and in person.

What advice would you give someone about to modify their Tesla?

Check that your insurer is happy with mods. Otherwise, you’ll have to wait 12 months like me before you can start changing things.

What’s next for the car?

A body kit, and maybe gloss black wheels.
