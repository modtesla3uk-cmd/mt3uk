Delete your Laps account

Deleting your Laps account removes your Laps profile, track sessions and the lap timer files you uploaded.

How to delete your Laps account

  1. Sign in at laps.mt3uk.com.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Press the Leave button (it is labelled Leave MT3UK), then confirm.

Once you confirm, you are signed out and your account is deleted.

If you can’t sign in

Use the contact page and ask us to delete your account. We’ll check it is you before we delete anything.

What gets deleted

  • Your Laps account, including your name, email address and nickname.
  • Your cars and their photos in My Garage.
  • Your track sessions, including the lap timer files you uploaded.
  • Your friends and messages.

Comments you have posted stay under the name they were posted with, unless you also ask us to remove them.

If you also joined on the website (mt3uk.com), leaving on Laps also deletes that website account, including your garage and photos there.

Deleting some data without leaving

You don’t have to delete your account to remove some of your data. You can delete individual track days, sessions, photos and cars from within Laps and My Garage.
