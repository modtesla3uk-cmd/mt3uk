01 / ABOUT

Built by owners, for owners

No dealership spin. Just people who've actually fitted the parts and driving the results.

MT3UK started as a small group of Tesla owners comparing notes on wheels, wraps and suspension setups - the kind of honest, hands-on advice you don't get in a showroom. It's grown into one of the largest communities for modified Tesla owners.

Whatever you're running — Model S, X, 3 or Y — and whatever stage your build is at, from a first set of wheels to a full track setup, you'll find people in the group who've done it, are doing it, or want to help you get there.

We keep the group free from bots, spam and probably most importantly "negativity" with a group of trusted admins on hand. Meet-ups across the UK and beyound and a shared respect for doing mods properly.
