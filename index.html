01 / ABOUT

Built by owners, for owners

No dealership spin. Just people who've actually fitted the parts and driving the results.

MT3UK started as a small group of Tesla owners comparing notes on wheels, wraps and suspension setups - the kind of honest, hands-on advice you don't get in a showroom. It's grown into one of the largest communities for modified Tesla owners.

Whatever you're running (Model S, 3, X or Y) and whatever stage your build is at, from a first set of wheels to a full track setup, you'll find people in the group who've done it, are doing it, or want to help you get there.

We keep the group free from bots, spam and probably most importantly "negativity" with a group of trusted admins on hand. Meet-ups across the UK and beyond and a shared respect for doing mods properly.

05 / TRACK DAYS

From sighting laps to setting your PB

Prep, upgrades and honest advice, for first timers and seasoned track goers alike.

This corner of MT3UK is dedicated to taking your Tesla on track, properly. Whether you're booking your very first track day or you've lost count of how many you've done, we're building this into the go-to spot for everything in between.

Expect prep checklists for first timers (tyres, brakes, cooling, what to actually pack), recommended upgrades that hold up under real track conditions rather than just Instagram, and unfiltered advice from members who've already made the mistakes so you don't have to.

It's early days, for now, consider this your pit lane. Got track photos, lap times or lessons learned? Share them in the Facebook group and they'll find a home here.
