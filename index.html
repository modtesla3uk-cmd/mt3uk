03 / ABOUT

Built by owners, for owners

MT3UK started as a small group of Tesla owners who wanted to make their cars unique. As aftermarket parts became more widespread, the mods went from a nice-to-have to something that's now almost essential for some. What began small has grown into one of the largest communities for modified Tesla owners.

Subtle look, front lip, full body kit, race-prepped track build - we love them all.

Whatever you're driving (Model S, 3, X or Y) and whatever stage your build is at, from a first set of wheels to a full track setup, you'll find people in the group who've done it, are doing it, or want to help you get there.

We keep the group free from bots, spam and negativity, with a team of trusted admins on hand. Regular meet-ups across the UK and beyond, and a shared respect for doing mods properly.

05 / TRACK DAYS Prep Guide →

From sighting laps to setting your PB

Prep, upgrades and honest advice, for first timers and seasoned track goers alike.

This corner of MT3UK is dedicated to taking your Tesla on track, properly. Whether you're booking your very first track day or you've lost count of how many you've done, we're building this into the go-to spot for everything in between. Read our Track Day Prep guide for OE limitations, model guidance and the upgrade path members follow, then the On The Day guide for insurance, breakdowns and charging once you've booked.

Expect recommended upgrades that hold up under real track conditions rather than just Instagram, and unfiltered advice from members who've already made the mistakes so you don't have to.

The photos below are shot at the circuits members actually book: Snetterton's long, fast Bentley Straight, Thruxton's sustained high-speed corners, Cadwell Park's tight, hilly technical layout, and Castle Combe's short, flat, high-average-speed lap. Each one loads brakes, tyres and suspension differently, which the Venues guide breaks down venue by venue.

Got track photos or lessons learned? Share them in the Facebook group and they'll find a home here.

06 / FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before you join.

Is MT3UK free to join?

Yes, it's a free Facebook group. No membership fees, no sign-up forms, just an admin-approved request to keep the bots and spam out.

Which Tesla models can I bring?

All of them. Model S, 3, X and Y owners are all welcome, whatever stage your build is at.

Do I need to already have mods to join?

No. Plenty of members join a stock Tesla looking for advice before they fit their first set of wheels or a wrap.

How do I get my build featured in the gallery?

Submit a photo and caption using the form in the gallery section, or post it in the Facebook group and an admin will add it for you.

Is there a track day for first timers?

Yes, we're gauging interest for a group track session. Check the events section above or ask in the Facebook group.
