Welcome

Here is how Laps works, in four steps.

  1. Export your sessions. Export the session from your lap timer, or save Track Mode data from your Tesla to a USB drive.
  2. Add a session. Upload the file on Sessions and choose your car.
  3. See every lap. Laps draws each lap on the map, with the times and where you gained or lost time.
  4. Share it. Share a session to put your fastest time on that track’s leaderboard.
