What is the Leaderboard?

Real times from EVs on tracks, drag strips and hill climbs, straight from members’ own lap timers. Every car’s fastest, with the tyres, pads and setup behind it.

  • A board for every track. Track days, sprints, hill climbs and drag strips, each layout on its own, with the fastest lap, run or quarter mile for every car.
  • Open a time to see the lap. Every row opens the session: the lap on the satellite map, speed, braking and cornering g, and the car’s tyres, pads and setup.
  • Compare like with like. Filter by model, conditions, driven wheels, tyres or kerb weight, or switch to Compare tyres and Compare pads to see what works best at each track.
  • Anyone can look. The boards are open to everyone. Adding your own laps is for early testers while we finish Laps; once the preview ends, every member can upload a session from their lap timer and go straight on the board.
