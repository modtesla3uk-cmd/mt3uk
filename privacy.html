Privacy notice

What MT3UK collects about you, why, who else handles it, how long it’s kept and your rights.

Last updated 2 October 2026

Who we are

MT3UK is a community for modified Tesla owners, run by Richard, its founder. Richard is responsible for your data (the “data controller”).

Questions, requests or worries about your data: email modtesla3uk@gmail.com.

Who MT3UK is for

MT3UK is for adults aged 18 and over. Please don’t join if you’re under 18. If we find an account belongs to someone under 18, we’ll delete it.

What we collect and why

  • Your account: your email address, first and last name, and a nickname if you choose one. We use them to sign you in, show who posted what and contact you about your account.
  • Your builds: the photos you add in My Garage, with the car’s name, mods and colour, to show in the gallery, the reel and the weekly vote.
  • Track sessions: if you upload a file from a lap timer, its laps, speeds and a trimmed GPS trace of the track, with the conditions and notes you add. The file itself is read on your device and isn’t uploaded. Sessions are private unless you choose to share them, which shows them on your build and that track’s leaderboard, and your notes are only ever shown to you. MT3UK’s admin can open a private session in a read-only view, without your notes, if you ask for help or to look into a fault. Each of those views is logged.
  • Messages between friends: the messages and photos you send are only visible to you and that friend (and to MT3UK if one is reported). Photos are kept for 90 days, and everything is deleted if either of you leaves MT3UK.
  • Your activity: comments, likes and votes. Your vote is kept against your account for 10 days, so each member has one vote a week. Other members see vote totals, never who voted.
  • Friends and private messages: who you’re friends with and the messages you send them, kept so they can be delivered. Nobody else reads them unless one is reported to us, when we read that message to decide what to do.
  • Passkeys: if you set one up, the public key your device creates and the device type (for example iPhone), so you can sign in without an email code. Your face or fingerprint never leaves your device, and we can’t see it.
  • Notifications: the notifications we show you, and, if you turn on phone notifications, the address your browser gives us to send them to.
  • Interview previews: which unpublished interview you opened and when, so we can see who has previewed it.
  • Things you send us: build claims, reports of comments or photos, reviews and messages through the Contact page.
  • Keeping the site safe: your IP address is used briefly to count requests and stop abuse. These counts are deleted within an hour.
  • Visitor numbers: we use Umami for anonymous page counts. It doesn’t use cookies or identify you.

Our lawful reasons

  • To give you what you signed up for (your account, My Garage, comments, friends and messages): this is needed to provide the service you asked for.
  • To keep MT3UK safe and running (moderation, stopping abuse, anonymous visitor numbers): our legitimate interest in a safe, working community.
  • Emails about likes, comments, friends and news from MT3UK: you can turn these off any time in your Profile. Sign-in codes always send, as you need them to get in.

What other people can see

  • Your nickname (or your first and last name if you haven’t set one) on your builds, comments and likes.
  • Your build photos, car name and mods, which are public on the site.
  • Track sessions only if you share them: the laps and trace on your build, and the session on that track’s leaderboard with your car and mods.
  • MT3UK’s admin, for private Track sessions: a read-only view for support, never your notes, and every view is logged.
  • Friends see your nickname and your builds, and the messages you send them.
  • Your email address is never shown to other members.

Who else handles your data

We never sell your data or share it for advertising. These services help run the site:

  • Cloudflare: runs the site’s server code, stores accounts, photos and messages, and sends our emails.
  • GitHub: hosts the website. New photos are logged there for us to check, with names and a masked email (for example ri•••@gmail.com).
  • Umami: anonymous visitor numbers, without cookies.
  • Open-Meteo: when you add a track session, we look up the weather at the track for that day so the air temperature fills itself in. Only the track’s location and the date and time are sent, nothing about you.
  • Esri: the satellite pictures under track session maps come from Esri’s World Imagery service. Your browser asks it for the pictures of the area being shown, so Esri sees that request and your internet address, as with any website picture. You can switch the pictures off with the layers button on the map.
  • Google Fonts: supplies the site’s fonts, so your browser’s IP address is sent to Google when pages load.
  • Ko-fi: handles shop orders under its own privacy policy. We see what you order and the details you give there.
  • Facebook: only if you follow our links to the MT3UK Facebook group.

Some of these companies may process data outside the UK, under their own safeguards for international transfers.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, builds, friends and messages: until you leave MT3UK or ask us to delete them.
  • Sign-in codes: 15 minutes. Interview preview access: 4 hours. Abuse-prevention counts: 1 hour.
  • Notifications: your latest 40. Messages: the latest 200 in each conversation.
  • When you leave, your account, builds, track sessions, friends and messages are deleted. Comments stay under the name they were posted with, unless you ask us to remove them too.

Your rights

You have the right to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to how we use it, and take a copy with you.

  • Correct your details: in your Profile.
  • Stop emails: in your Profile.
  • Delete your account: use Leave MT3UK in your Profile.
  • A copy of your data, or removing your comments: email modtesla3uk@gmail.com. We’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and your browser

MT3UK doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps a few things for the site to work as you’d expect: your sign-in, the anonymous id used for likes and votes, your bookmarks, the order of your homepage tiles and which messages you’ve closed. Signing out, or clearing your browser’s site data, removes them.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your data, we’ll update this page and the date at the top, and tell members about big changes through Messages in their Profile.
