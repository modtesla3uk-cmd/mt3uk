How do I set up a passkey?
  1. Sign in once with an email code, above.
  2. Tap Set up a passkey when it’s offered, or go to Profile, Security, Add a passkey on this device.
  3. Next time, tap Sign in with a passkey and use Face ID, your fingerprint or screen lock.

Each phone or computer needs its own passkey, unless it’s saved to your iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager.
