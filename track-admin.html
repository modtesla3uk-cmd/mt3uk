    Sign-in and sign-up

    How signing in and joining works on laps.mt3uk.com. A sign-in is kept per address, so the emailed link always comes back to laps.mt3uk.com when it was asked for there.

    On: Laps has its own Sign in page (laps-signin.html) and the emails come from Laps by MT3UK and talk about Laps. Off: Laps uses the MT3UK Sign Up / Sign In page and emails.

    On: joining on Laps makes an MT3UK member, who can also like and comment on mt3uk.com. Off: a Laps-only account, which signs in on Laps but not on mt3uk.com until they join MT3UK there. MT3UK members can always sign in to Laps with the same email.

    New Laps sign-ups

    Everyone who joins on laps.mt3uk.com, newest first (the latest 100), with whether they became an MT3UK member or have a Laps-only account. While Laps is an early preview each one also waits on the Early access panel, which has its bell item and email. Once Early access is open to all, each new sign-up is counted on the bell and emailed to MT3UK instead. Clear takes one off this list; their account is not changed.

    Members' sessions

    New sessions as they are saved, requests to edit a map, and finding a member's sessions.

    New sessions

    Each session a member saves is listed here, counted on the bell and emailed to MT3UK, with its details and a link. The New sessions switch at the top turns all of that off (the Email switch still covers the email). Clear takes a session off the list once you have seen it.

    Line editing

    A member cannot move the start and finish lines on a session they have saved. They press Request Edit Map on the session and wait here. Allow lets them edit that one map (they are emailed). When they send a change you are emailed what the lines and time were and would be, and nothing changes until you Accept it or Undo it. Accepting works the time out again from the saved readings and shows you the result first. On a listed course it then makes the new lines the course's official lines and re-times every session at that track (a time that moves by over 10% is held back), so it changes other members' times too. Revoke switches their editing off once they are done.

    Member sessions

    Find a member by email to open their sessions, even private ones. You get a read-only view without their notes, and each view is logged below. Members are told in the privacy page. Links open on this browser once you have entered the admin key. A session at a track we do not list yet can be renamed here; one at a listed track takes its name from the Tracks panel.

    One session

    Paste a session link or id. It says which board the session belongs on, whether its car is on it, and what is stopping it if not.

    All leaderboard problems

    Every shared session that is not on a leaderboard for a reason that can be fixed, and any board the track list is not counting, so it is hidden. Private sessions and cars shown with a faster session are not problems.

    Rebuild and re-time

    Leaderboard places keep each car's best for every mix of conditions and tyres, and its track-relevant parts. Places made before that are brought up to date here: it goes through the cars a few at a time.

      Driven wheels

      Every vehicle with sessions, and the wheels it drives (FWD, RWD or AWD) as worked out from its make, model and version, or as its owner set. Set or correct one here: it is saved to the vehicle, stamped on its sessions and put on its leaderboard rows. Vehicles we cannot tell come first. A default for a whole model is set on the Vehicles panel.

      Brake pads

      The brake pad makes and compounds members pick from on the Add page, and for each compound what its maker publishes: its use, friction level (μ) and working temperature range. Compare pads on the leaderboard shows these as the maker's figures beside what Laps has measured. data/pads.json is the starting list; changes made here are stored by the worker on top of it and used straight away.

      Vehicles

      The vehicle makes and models offered when a car or bike is added. The Model box suggests a make's models and members can still type anything. data/vehicles.json is the starting list; changes made here are stored by the worker on top of it and used straight away. A make is a car or a bike, and the same name can be both. Edit renames a make, lists its models and, for each model, its variants (the Version choices in My Garage). Each model's row below also has the Version box's rule, saved as soon as it is switched: Required (the member must pick one; it is optional otherwise) and Free text (a Type it in choice lets them type any version). Each car model shows the wheels it drives (FWD, RWD or AWD) as worked out from its name, and a Default drop-down: a default is what the model's vehicles take unless the version says otherwise (Long Range AWD, Taycan 4S, Carrera 4) or the vehicle was set by hand, and setting one stamps the sessions of every vehicle of that model and refreshes its leaderboard rows.

      Members' cars

      Every car and bike in members' garages with its settings: type, make, model, version, year and driven wheels, as the owner set them in My Garage. Check and change any of them here (each drop-down offers the vehicle list's choices and Type it in for anything else): Save writes them to the car, stamps its sessions and refreshes its leaderboard rows, as if the owner had saved them. Cars with no model come first. A record with no owner, no live photo naming it and no sessions (its photos were deleted or moved to another car) is marked stale and can be removed; nothing else is deleted.

      Track sessions sharing

      The picture a shared Track sessions link shows in WhatsApp, Facebook and the rest. Draw one from a saved session (its map, times and g chart) or upload a photo, give it a caption, and either rotate through them a week at a time or pick one. Previews update within a few minutes of a change; a link already sent in a chat keeps its old picture.
