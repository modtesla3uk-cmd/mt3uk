What are Sessions?

Your track days, sprints, hill climbs and drag runs from your lap timer file (RaceBox, VBOX, AiM, Harry’s LapTimer, TrackAddict and most phone apps). Upload it and Laps does the rest.

  • Every lap mapped. Compare any two laps on the satellite map, with speed, acceleration, braking and cornering g, and see where you gained or lost time, corner by corner.
  • Tyres and brake pads. Record the tyres and pads you ran on each session, from their make and model lists. Laps keeps them with your times, beside the makers’ own figures: wet grip, noise and EV rating for tyres, and the working temperature range for pads.
  • Compare tyres and pads. On the Leaderboard, see which tyres and pads are quickest at each track, with the best and typical times, peak braking g and brake temperatures, and a warning when pads ran hotter than their range.
  • Your car and its mods. Sessions are linked to your car in My Garage, so its mods, kerb weight and driven wheels sit beside your times, and you can see what each change did.
  • The Leaderboard. Share a session to put your car on the board for that track, or keep it to yourself. Filter by model, conditions, driven wheels, air temperature and weight.
